Frame work with OPALE ​

An OPALE story is prepared before it is coded: analysis, framing, specifications, plan, then execution. You come here when you want to set out the problem with your agent, record decisions and questions, or, on the contrary, fix a defect without ceremony.

What you ask the agent ​

💬 You ask your agent

« Create the framing documents for story 003 of epic 012. »

→ The agent callseffortless_cadrage_docs_scaffold
  • epic_id = 012
  • story_id = 003
💬 You ask your agent

« Record, on story 003 of epic 012, the decision "Export as CSV" in the framing phase, because Excel must open the exports; consequence: handle the encoding; ruled-out alternative: JSON. »

→ The agent callseffortless_decision_add
  • epic_id = 012
  • story_id = 003
  • title = Export as CSV
  • context = Excel must open the exports
  • decision = Export as CSV
  • consequences = Handle the encoding
  • rejected_alternatives = JSON
  • phase = P-cadrage
💬 You ask your agent

« On story 003 of epic 012, ask the authentication question in the framing phase: context, the application will be multi-user; impact, nothing moves forward without an answer; suggestion, OAuth2. »

→ The agent callseffortless_question_ask
  • epic_id = 012
  • story_id = 003
  • question = Which authentication?
  • context = The application will be multi-user
  • impact = Nothing moves forward without an answer
  • suggestion = OAuth2
  • phase = P-cadrage

What the agent does ​

Prepare a story's documents ​

  1. effortless_cadrage_docs_scaffold with epic_id and story_id: creates the story's missing OPALE documents in blueprints/ and returns the files created. Documents already there are left alone.
  2. effortless_cadrage_layer_next: at project level, tells which framing layer to write next among the four root blueprints.

Move through the phases ​

An OPALE story goes through five phases: O-analyse, P-cadrage, A-specs, L-plan, E-execute.

  1. effortless_phase_eligibility: read-only. Tells whether the current phase's required documents exist and which phase comes next.
  2. effortless_phase_next: moves to the next phase, with the date set by the server. The engine redoes the check on its side.
  3. effortless_story_reloop, when the framing has to be redone: target_phase names a phase earlier than the current one.

As long as the story is before the E-execute phase, a task cannot move to In progress (see Run the work).

Record decisions ​

  1. effortless_decision_add: a decision (ADR) attached to a story. The agent fills in epic_id, story_id, title, context, decision, consequences, rejected_alternatives and the OPALE phase concerned. The engine returns the DEC-NN identifier.
  2. effortless_decisions_list: all the project's decisions, with their epic and story of origin.

Ask and resolve questions ​

  1. effortless_question_ask: an open question on a story (question, context, impact, suggestion, phase). The engine returns Q-NN.
  2. effortless_question_resolve: resolves it with its answer.
  3. effortless_questions_list: all the project's questions.

Big open questions (BQO) ​

A BQO is a question asked at project level, not on a story.

  1. effortless_bqo_ask: records it.
  2. effortless_bqo_list: lists them.
  3. effortless_bqo_resolve: answers it (question_id, answer).
  4. effortless_bqo_graduate: if it deserves a project of its own, copies it into an existing epic (epic_id). It does not create an epic; the question is no longer answered at project level but from that epic.

Story drafts ​

To break an epic down without creating the stories right away.

  1. effortless_draft_add: a draft (title, summary, kind opale or fix, proposed tasks, rationale, dependencies) in the active epic or the named one.
  2. effortless_draft_update: edits a draft; anything not passed stays unchanged.
  3. effortless_draft_list: lists an epic's drafts.
  4. effortless_draft_submit: submits it for your review.
  5. effortless_draft_validate: turns it into a real story and returns the identifier created.
  6. effortless_draft_reject: sets it aside.

The agent submits; you are the one who says validate or reject.

Proposals on a story ​

A proposal is a summary attached to a story, waiting for your verdict.

  1. effortless_proposal_add: adds it (pending) to the active story, or to story_id.
  2. effortless_proposal_validate: validates it.
  3. effortless_proposal_reject: rejects it.

The short route: a fix ​

For a defect to fix right now, the agent does not need to frame anything.

  1. effortless_story_start with kind: "fix": the story needs no framing and opens directly in the execution phase. With activate: true, it becomes the active story.
  2. effortless_task_add then effortless_task_update: the rest goes as for a normal story (Run the work).

Acceptance scenarios and proofs ​

  1. effortless_recipe_maturity: read-only. Sorts the acceptance scenarios by maturity (stable, candidate, beta, planned), returns what remains to be done and the scenarios blocked by a gap. Optionally limited to one epic_id or story_id.
  2. effortless_interruption_proof: proves that a write order survives a real interruption. The engine kills the declared command (SIGKILL) at each position it announces, then runs the state check. proof_id names a proof you declared in effortless.json under settings.interruption_proofs; positions restricts the cuts (by default, all of them).

What you get ​

  • Framing documents in blueprints/, in Markdown, to read in your editor.
  • Decisions and questions in the Knowledge screen, with their origin.
  • Stories, their phase and their drafts in the Backlog (the Drafts, Proposal and Reloop blocks on the epic and story pages).
  • Open questions and unverified acceptance scenarios under Waiting for you on the Dashboard.
